LA NOSTRA STORIA

We grow, we preserve

AGRUMEA is a family farm under the Sicilian sun. Citrus groves, almond trees and vegetable gardens fill the land; the kitchen sits in the middle of it. What the season gives in abundance, we preserve at the right moment, in small copper pans, so winter tastes of summer.

Grown here

Fruit and vegetables from our own groves and gardens, picked at full ripeness.

Small batches

Each preserve is cooked in small pans and jarred by hand, never in industrial runs.

Nothing wasted

Peel, pulp and seeds all find their way back into the kitchen or the soil.
